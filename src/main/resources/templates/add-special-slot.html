What are Special Slots?

  • Use after Auto Generate to pin fixed entries like INTERNSHIP, AUDIT COURSE, T&P, LIBRARY, SEMINAR.
  • Batch field is optional — leave blank for the whole division, or enter e.g. T1, S2 for a specific batch.
  • These entries appear in the timetable grid and PDF export alongside auto-generated slots.
